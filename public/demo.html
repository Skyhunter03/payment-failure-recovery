Or jump straight to a customer screen

NOT_DEBITED
No money left your account.
Pre-authorisation decline (incorrect OTP), no acquirer reference — the payment never touched the bank.
DEBITED_REVERSAL_EXPECTED
Money left, and it's on its way back.
A real signed webhook with an acquirer reference (RRN) present — see the note below on why this isn't triggered from a live test card.
CONFIRMING
We're checking with your bank.
Infra failure (issuer down), no reference yet — genuinely uncertain, so no reversal date is invented.

The DEBITED_REVERSAL_EXPECTED screen is driven by a real, correctly-signed payment.failed webhook with a production-shaped payload (an acquirer reference present) — already verified end-to-end and persisted in the live database, not a mock. Razorpay's test mode doesn't route through a real bank, so it has no acquirer RRN to hand back on a live test-card failure; there's no test card that naturally produces this state.
